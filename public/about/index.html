Read your books. Keep what stays with you.

Noesis is a reading app for the books you own and borrow. It opens EPUB and PDF files right in your browser, keeps your highlights and notes in one place you can search, and helps you find what to read next.

Read what you own

  • EPUB and PDF books open in a reader with your choice of type, spacing and theme, including a read-aloud voice.
  • Your books can sit in your own Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox and come with you to every device.
  • Books you read in Kindle, Apple Books or elsewhere sit on the same shelf and open where you read them.

Library loans

  • Connect Libby and your loans arrive with their covers and due dates. Read opens the loan in Libby.
  • Paper loans from any library or friend keep their due dates too, with a link to renew.
  • See which nearby libraries have a book, on the shelf or in Libby.

Keep what you read

  • Highlight a passage and add a thought. Everything you keep lands in your Second Brain, ready to search.
  • Bring in highlights from a Kindle or Libby.
  • Noesis Notes is a notebook for paper books and anything you read away from the app.

Rabbit Holes

Start from a question you want to understand. Each one comes with books, articles and videos to explore, and you can go deeper, one question at a time, for as long as you like.

Private by design

Noesis works without an account, and your books and notes stay on your device. An account adds sync between your devices. Nothing you read is sold, used for ads, or used to train AI models. Read the privacy policy.
