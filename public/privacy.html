Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Noesis is a reading and learning app operated by Proairetos ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Noesis handles, where it goes, and the choices you have. We keep it short because the app is designed to keep your material on your own device and in storage you control.

In short: your books and notes stay in your browser unless you sign in or connect cloud storage. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your content to train AI models.

What stays on your device

Books you import (EPUB and PDF files), their extracted text, your reading position, notes, highlights, learning paths, review cards, and preferences are stored in your browser (IndexedDB and local storage). We cannot see this information unless you choose to sync it. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Connected cloud storage (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox)

If you choose to connect a provider, Noesis asks for the narrowest access it can use and only reads and writes files it creates for syncing your library:

  • Google Drive: the drive.file scope, which limits Noesis to files it creates. It cannot see your other Drive files.
  • Microsoft OneDrive: the app folder (Files.ReadWrite.AppFolder) and basic profile (User.Read).
  • Dropbox: a dedicated app folder, with permission to read and write files and read file metadata.

The access token for a provider is kept in your browser. It is not sent to or stored on Noesis servers, and Noesis never receives your provider password. Your synced files live in your own provider account, and you can disconnect at any time in Noesis (Cloud Backup) or revoke access from your Google, Microsoft, or Dropbox account settings.

Noesis's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use Google Drive data only to sync your own library to your own Drive. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and no person reads it except as needed for security, to comply with law, or with your explicit consent.

AI features (Noema)

When you ask Noema a question or request review questions, Noesis sends your question, the nearby reading text or passage, and relevant notes to our server, which forwards them to Google's Gemini API to generate a response. This happens only when you use those features. Gemini processes the content under Google's terms. We do not use your content to train AI models. AI answers can be wrong, so check important information against the source.

Services that help us run Noesis

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and server code and processes request data such as IP addresses.
  • Supabase provides sign-in, note storage, and library file storage for signed-in users.
  • Google (Gemini API) generates Noema answers and review questions.
  • Open-Meteo receives the city name or coordinates you choose for the weather forecast. If you use “Detect my location”, your browser asks for permission first, and BigDataCloud may receive your coordinates to turn them into a place name. Neither is contacted until you set up the forecast.
  • Open Library, Project Gutenberg, OpenAlex, Crossref, and the Internet Archive receive your search terms when you use Explore. Files you import from them are fetched through our server, which only retrieves content from approved free-resource sites.

These providers have their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

How long we keep information

Account data, notes, and synced books are kept until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. We do not store your Noema questions after the response is generated. Basic server logs are kept by our hosting provider for a limited time.

Your choices

  • Delete books, notes, paths, and review cards inside the app at any time.
  • Use Noesis without an account; your material then stays only in your browser.
  • Disconnect any cloud provider, or revoke access from the provider's own settings.
  • Export your library as a ZIP from Cloud Backup.
  • Ask us to delete your account and the data stored with it by emailing the address below.

Security

Connections to Noesis use HTTPS, and signed-in data is protected by per-user access rules in our database and storage. No online service is perfectly secure, so keep your password private and use a unique one.

Children

Noesis is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you in the app.
